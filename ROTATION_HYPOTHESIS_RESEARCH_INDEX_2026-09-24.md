## RESEARCH COMPLETED

This research session has completed a systematic test of the hypothesis: **Could the BoM author have used Lahontan-derived maps (especially Moll) but with ROTATED cardinal directions?**

### What Was Tested
1. **Moll 1732 map** in four orientations:
   - Standard orientation (North at top)
   - 90° clockwise rotation (East becomes North)
   - 90° counterclockwise rotation (West becomes North)
   - 180° rotation (complete inversion)

2. **Comparison against BoM geographic features**:
   - River flow direction (must be E-W)
   - Territorial divisions (must be N-S)
   - Narrow passage geometry (must run N-S)
   - River direction specificity (E→W vs. W→E)
   - Coastal settlement patterns

3. **Statistical and mechanistic assessment**:
   - Confidence levels for each feature
   - Critical gaps in hypothesis
   - Plausible access mechanisms
   - Base-rate analysis needs
