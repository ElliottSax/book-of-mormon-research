## CRITICAL RESEARCH GAPS (Must Fill These)

### GAP #1: MORSE INCLUDES COMOROS?
**Question**: Does Morse's *American Universal Geography* (any edition 1805-1823) mention "Comoros"?

**Why it matters**:
- Morse was THE mass-audience geography text in 1820s America
- 85% plausible Rigdon had access to Morse (per existing research)
- If yes, creates direct transmission pathway from text → Smith/Rigdon → BoM

**How to check**:
1. Go to HathiTrust Digital Library (https://www.hathitrust.org)
2. Search for "Jedidiah Morse American Universal Geography"
3. Try editions: 1805, 1809, 1813, 1819, 1823
4. Use full-text search for keyword "Comoros" or "Comore"
5. If found, note: edition, page number, exact text, context

**Timeline**: 30 minutes to 2 hours

---

### GAP #2: WHEN DID "MORONI" BECOME CAPITAL NAME?
**Question**: When was "Moroni" established as capital of Comoros, and was it known in English by 1820?

**Why it matters**:
- If "Moroni" named AFTER 1830 (after BoM), then identical naming is coincidence, not transmission
- If "Moroni" named BEFORE 1820 and appeared in English texts, then transmission is plausible

**How to check**:
1. Research French colonial history of Comoros
2. Find when Moroni became administrative center/capital
3. Check English-language texts (maps, encyclopedias) for when "Moroni" appears in Comoros context
4. Document: earliest English mention of "Moroni" as Comoros capital

**Timeline**: 5-7 days of research

**Research resources**:
- Encyclopedia Britannica (check multiple editions)
- HathiTrust and Archive.org for historical geography texts
- Academic databases on French colonial history
- Historical atlases showing Comoros

### GAP #3: NAME FREQUENCY — HOW RARE WAS "MORONI"?
**Question**: How common was the name "Moroni" in English-speaking world 1800-1830?

**Why it matters**:
- If "Moroni" was a known English name, coincident naming is more plausible
- If "Moroni" was rare/exotic, identical naming becomes more striking

**How to check**:
1. Check historical name databases: FamilySearch, Ancestry.com, etc.
2. Search 1800-1830 American ship captains' logs for crew members named "Moroni"
3. Search military records for any "Moroni" individuals
4. Check Italian/Mediterranean genealogy sources (if Moroni is Italian origin)
5. Search period newspapers for personal name mentions

**Timeline**: 7-14 days of research

**Outcome expected**: 
- Rare name: "~0-5 instances in major databases" → supports hypothesis (+15 points)
- Common name: "~50+ instances" → undermines uniqueness of naming (-15 points)
