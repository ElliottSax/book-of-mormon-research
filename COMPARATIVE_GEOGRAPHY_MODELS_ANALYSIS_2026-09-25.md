## PART II: COMPETING GEOGRAPHIC MODELS

### Model 1: MESOAMERICA MODEL
**Primary Proponents**: BYU scholars (Sorenson, Spilsbury); LDS apologetics mainstream  
**Geographic Claim**: BoM lands = Yucatan Peninsula + Central America + Mexico

**Territory Bounds**:
- Land Southward = Mesoamerica proper (Mexico/Guatemala highlands)
- Land Northward = Interior Mexico / southwestern North America
- Narrow neck = Isthmus of Tehuantepec (roughly 200 km)
- River Sidon = Rio Usumacinta or Rio Chixoy
- Nephite heartland = Highlands of Guatemala

**Feature Claims**:

| Feature | Mesoamerica Explanation | Text Match | Geographic Match |
|---------|------------------------|-----------|-----------------|
| **F1** Cardinal seas | Modern cardinal directions on standard map | ~ Partial | ~ Requires reorientation |
| **F2** River dividing | Rio Usumacinta divides territories | ✓ Strong | ✓ Strong |
| **F3** River E-W flow | Usumacinta flows NW not E-W | ✗ Poor | ✗ Doesn't match |
| **F4** Narrow neck | Isthmus of Tehuantepec (200 km) | ~ Partial | ✗ Too wide (4+ days) |
| **F5** Lands surrounded by water | Coastal on 2-3 sides, not 4 | ~ Partial | ~ Incomplete |
| **F6** Land division | Clear geographic division | ✓ Strong | ✓ Strong |
| **F7** Wilderness barriers | Mountain ranges present | ✓ Strong | ✓ Strong |
| **F8** Coastal emphasis | Maya coastal cities exist | ✓ Strong | ✓ Strong |
| **F9** Inland capital | Zarahemla = Izapa or Kaminaljuyu | ~ Weak | ~ Speculative |
| **F10** Territorial subdivisions | Many Maya city-states | ✓ Strong | ✓ Strong |

**Score**: 7/15 features (47%) = **Minimum threshold, barely credible**

**Strengths**:
- Rich archaeological parallels
- Well-established scholarly tradition
- Multiple city sites could correspond to BoM locations
- Wilderness barriers (mountains) match

**Critical Weaknesses**:
- River Sidon flows NW, not E-W (contradicts F3)
- Narrow neck is 200+ km, not "day and a half" (contradicts F4)
- Cardinal seas naming requires modern reorientation, not historical mapping (contradicts F1)
- Travel times don't match realistic distances
- E-W river flow impossible in this geography

## PART IV: TEXT EXPLANATION TEST

### Test Case 1: Alma 22:27-32 (Comprehensive Geography)

**Key Passage**:  
> "And now, it was only the distance of a day and a half's journey from the east to the west sea; and thus the land of Nephi and the land of Zarahemla were nearly surrounded by water, all save a small neck of land, which joined them together. And it came to pass that the Nephites had inhabited the land Bountiful even from the east to the west sea, and thus the Nephites in their wisdom, with their guards and their armies, had hemmed in the Lamanites..."

**What the passage requires**:
1. Two distinct lands separated by narrow connector
2. Connected lands nearly surrounded by water (4-sided bounding)
3. Land-to-land travel distance: exactly 1.5 days
4. Distinct east and west seas
5. Defensive capability (hemming in enemies)

**Model Explanations**:

**Mesoamerica**: 
- Yucatan + Guatemala highlands connected by Isthmus of Tehuantepec
- Problem: Isthmus is ~200 km (4+ days), not 1.5 days
- Problem: Doesn't naturally "hem in" in geographic sense
- Problem: "East sea" and "west sea" are modern compass directions, not period naming
- **Explanation Quality**: POOR (fails on most specific geographic claims)

**North American**:
- Ohio Valley + Great Lakes via portage/narrow passage
- Problem: What is the "west sea"? Great Lakes aren't a sea
- Problem: What is the "east sea"? Atlantic is distant and not closely bounded
- Problem: 1.5 day journey from "east to west" unclear geographically
- **Explanation Quality**: POOR (unclear geography, no obvious "seas")

**European/Panama**:
- Central America + northern South America connected by Isthmus of Panama
- Panama isthmus is 82 km, realistically 1.5 days' journey ✓
- Nearly surrounded by water: Caribbean (east) + Pacific (west) + Gulf of Panama ✓
- "East sea" = Atlantic (Eastern reference from Europe) ✓
- "West sea" = Pacific (Western reference from Europe) ✓
- Defensible position naturally hegemonic ✓
- **Explanation Quality**: EXCELLENT (matches all specific claims exactly)
