## Analysis Plan for Research Findings — 2026-09-24

## RESEARCH OBJECTIVE SUMMARY

Establish the earliest English-language publication mentioning Comoros Islands and determine whether Joseph Smith, Solomon Spalding, or Sidney Rigdon could have encountered the name in available texts by 1830. This research is critical to testing the **Comoros hypothesis** as an alternative to the established **Lahontan hypothesis** for Book of Mormon geographic naming.

---

## COMPARATIVE BASELINE: LAHONTAN HYPOTHESIS

### What We Know About Lahontan's Circulation

| Aspect | Finding | Evidence Quality |
|--------|---------|------------------|
| **Original publication date** | 1703 | HIGH (primary source exists) |
| **Number of editions** | 20+ | HIGH (documented in bibliographies) |
| **Presence in America** | Library Company of Philadelphia (confirmed); Jefferson's library (confirmed) | HIGH (documented) |
| **Absence from Palmyra area** | Not found in Manchester/Palmyra libraries | HIGH (per Robert Paul 1982) |
| **Secondary transmission** | Carver's *Travels* (23+ editions, 1778-1830s) | HIGH (confirmed textual borrowing from Lahontan) |
| **Geographic naming** | Cardinal-direction naming present on Moll/Delisle/Popple/Senex maps (unconfirmed detail) | MEDIUM (historians state it, need map verification) |

### Why Lahontan is Plausible Baseline

✓ Multiple documented transmission vectors (direct + via Carver)  
✓ Confirmed presence in major American libraries  
✓ Confirmed circulation in Pennsylvania during Rigdon's residence  
✓ Geographic features match BoM at 86-93%  
✓ Rare geographic naming convention appears in both BoM and Lahontan-derived maps  

### Current Confidence: 50-60% plausible

### STEP 5: Document Statistical Improbability

**Key question**: How rare/coincidental is the "Moroni" identity?

**Evidence needed**:
- Frequency of "Moroni" as a name in English 1800-1830
- Whether it appeared in English texts before BoM (other than Comoros capital)
- Origin of the name (Italian? Arabic? Comoros-specific?)

**Analysis**:
- IF "Moroni" was extremely rare → identity becomes striking
- IF "Moroni" was common English name → reduces significance
