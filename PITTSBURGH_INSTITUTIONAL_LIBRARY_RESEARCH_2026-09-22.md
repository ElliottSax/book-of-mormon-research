## RESEARCH METHODOLOGY

### What Counts as Documentary Evidence
✅ **Primary sources**:
- Surviving library catalogs dated 1822-1830
- Donation/acquisition records with specific titles and dates
- Binding records (indicate book ownership/processing)
- Membership lists showing Rigdon or his documented associates

✅ **Secondary sources**:
- Published institutional histories citing library holdings
- Academic studies citing early catalogs
- Historical surveys with quoted catalog entries

❌ **Insufficient evidence**:
- "Institutions likely held X" (statistical probability without docs)
- "Rigdon could have accessed" (without documented access)
- General library history without specific title/date evidence

### Documentation Standards
For each potential holding, record:
1. **Specific title**: Exact edition (author, publication year, edition)
2. **Acquisition date**: When acquired (or date range)
3. **Source document**: Catalog page, donation letter, binding record
4. **Confidence level**: Based on document clarity and dating
5. **Rigdon connection**: Evidence he could have accessed it
