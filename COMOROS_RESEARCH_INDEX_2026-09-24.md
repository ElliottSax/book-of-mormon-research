
## REPORTING STANDARDS

All research findings should document:

1. **Source**: Exactly where information came from
2. **Quote**: Verbatim text if available
3. **Date**: Publication date of source
4. **Availability**: How others can verify (URL, library, archive location)
5. **Confidence**: HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW assessment
6. **Implication**: What this means for the hypothesis

### Example Reporting
```
FINDING: Britannica 1st edition (1768) mentions Comoros

Source: Britannica, 1st edition, Vol. 2, p. 347
Quote: "The Comoro Islands, situated in the Indian Ocean between Madagascar and the African coast, are known to European traders as a source of spices and trade goods."
Date: 1768-1771
Availability: HathiTrust Digital Library (https://www.hathitrust.org)
Confidence: HIGH (primary source verified)
Implication: Establishes English knowledge of Comoros by 1768, suggesting knowledge available by 1820s America
```
