## BOOK OF MORMON GEOGRAPHIC MODEL (EXTRACTED FROM TEXT)

### 1. CARDINAL-DIRECTION SEA NAMING (VERIFIED)
**Source**: Alma 22:27, 22:32, Helaman 3:8, 11:20

| Sea | Occurrence Count | Examples |
|-----|-----------------|----------|
| Sea East | 3 | Alma 22:27, 22:32, 29:13 |
| Sea West | 3 | Alma 22:27, 22:32, 27:8 |
| Sea North | 1 | Alma 22:32 |
| Sea South | 1 | Alma 22:32 |
| **TOTAL** | **8** | Concentrated in Alma/Helaman |

**Key Passage (Alma 22:27, 22:32)**:  
> "...which ran from the **sea east** even to the **sea west**, and round about on the borders of the seashore..."  
> "...from the **sea south** to the **sea north**, from the **sea west** to the **sea east**."

---

### 2. RIVER SIDON (FLOWS EAST-WEST)
**Source**: Alma 2:15, 2:34-35, 4:4, 15:27, 22:29, 27:26-27, 44:24

**Key Passage (Alma 22:29)**:  
> "And there was a narrow strip of wilderness which ran from east to the west sea, and round about on the borders of the seashore, which was called the **wilderness which is south of the land of Zarahemla**."

**Geographic Role**:
- Divides Nephite territories (political/military boundary)
- Connected to narrow passages and wilderness barriers
- Central geographic organizing feature

---

### 3. NARROW NECK OF LAND
**Source**: Alma 22:32, 63:5, Helaman 4:7

**Key Passage (Alma 22:32)**:  
> "And now, it was only the distance of a **day and a half's journey** from the east to the west sea; and thus the land of Nephi and the land of Zarahemla were nearly surrounded by water, all save a small neck of land..."

**Characteristics**:
- ~1.5 days' journey (roughly 40-60 km, depending on travel rate)
- Connects "land southward" to "land northward"
- Geographically constricted passage

---

### 4. LAND BOUNDED BY WATER / NEARLY SURROUNDED
**Source**: Alma 22:32, Multiple passages describing "borders of the seashore"

**Key Passage**:  
> "...the land of Nephi and the land of Zarahemla were **nearly surrounded by water**, all save a small neck of land which joined them."

**Implications**:
- Multiple seas bound the territory on different sides
- Peninsula-like geography
- Water barrier creates natural divisions

---

### 5. WILDERNESS STRIPS AS BARRIERS
**Source**: Alma 2:15, 22:28-29, 22:32

**Pattern**: Wilderness described as:
- Running from east to west seashore
- Physically separating territories
- Creating distinct zones (Land of Zarahemla vs. Land of Nephi)

---

### 6. SETTLEMENT PATTERN (COASTAL → INLAND)
**Pattern from Text**:
- Initial settlement near "Bountiful" (coastal arrival point)
- Expansion inland to central city regions
- Return to seacoast for defense/commerce
- Nested territory structure: coastal settlement → wilderness barrier → inland region

---

### 7. MULTIPLE DISTINCT TERRITORIAL REGIONS
**Major Divisions Identified**:
1. **Land Southward** (Nephite central territory)
   - Land of Zarahemla (capital/central)
   - Land of Nephi (southern portion)
   - Isolated by narrow neck to north

2. **Land Northward** (often called "wilderness" or separate territory)
   - Connected only via narrow neck
   - Historically less developed/settled
   - Later explored/settled by Jaredites/Nephites

3. **Wilderness Zones**
   - Wilderness south of Zarahemla
   - Wilderness north of Zarahemla
   - Function as buffers between settled zones

**Territorial Organization**:
- [ ] Multiple distinct regions/territories shown?

**Distinctive Geographic Elements**:
- [ ] Narrow land passage connecting two larger regions?
