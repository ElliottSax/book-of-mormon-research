## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The Book of Mormon research project is investigating the **Comoros Islands hypothesis** as an alternative explanation for geographic place-names in the sacred text. This hypothesis focuses on two striking onomastic parallels:

1. **"Moroni" as perfect identity**: Book of Mormon features "Captain Moroni" (Alma 43:16+) and later "Angel Moroni" (Moroni 10:34). The capital city of the Comoros Islands is also named "Moroni" — identical spelling, not coincidental similarity.

2. **"Cumorah/Comoros" as phonetic similarity**: The Book of Mormon's Hill Cumorah (primary religious geography) has 0.440 composite phonetic similarity to "Comoros," which **exceeds the similarity of Lahontan/Liahona baseline (0.336)** and contains a NYSIIS phonetic code match.

**Current research status**: Testing whether "Comoros" was known to English speakers and accessible to Joseph Smith, Solomon Spalding, or Sidney Rigdon by 1830.
