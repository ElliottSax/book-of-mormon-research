| Model | Explains this convention | Source documentation |

### 2. East-West River Flow (Alma 22:29)
BoM text: "a narrow strip of wilderness which ran from east to the west sea"

**Why this is impossible in real geography**: No major river system crosses a continent E-W. The continental divide prevents this.

**But it's documented in 18th-century FICTIONAL geography**: Lahontan's fictional "Long River" (Rivière Longue) is shown E-W on maps:
- Visible on Moll's 23+ editions
- Documented in Carver's *Travels* (1778)
- Accepted by geographic scholars as Lahontan's invention

**Verdict**: The European/Panama model explains an impossible geographic feature by reference to documented source material. Other models simply have no explanation.

### 3. Source Availability + Pittsburgh Access (Lahontan → Carver → Rigdon)

**Documented transmission chain**:
1. Lahontan publishes *New Voyages* (1703) — 25+ editions by 1763
2. Carver borrows from Lahontan (documented in Bourne 1906 scholarship)
