## CROSS-VECTOR SYNTHESIS STRATEGY

Once individual vectors produce results, test these hypotheses:

1. **The Carver pathway** (not Lahontan): If evidence emerges that Rigdon had documented access to Carver editions, this is stronger than Lahontan (Carver circulated more widely).

2. **Campbell as intermediary**: If Campbell's library/reading lists include Carver or travel narratives, and Rigdon had documented access to Campbell's materials, this creates a plausible transmission chain.

3. **Print evidence as confirmation**: If early BoM printing shows type/compositor patterns consistent with travel-narrative source materials being available in the printing shop, this provides indirect corroboration.

4. **The Smith family angle**: If the Hale family (Emma's parents) had documented book collections including geography/travel texts, this creates an alternate pathway through Emma rather than Rigdon alone.

5. **Critics' hearsay**: If 19th-century ex-Mormon or critical sources claim Rigdon read widely or had "suspicious geographic knowledge," this is contemporaneous evidence (not later speculation).
